Data rules used on this page

  • Price rule: only sales from $10,000 to $5,000,000 are included. The raw data has impossible prices (one sale at $0) and a few sales above $5 million (up to $81.5 million).
  • Days on market = contract_date − list_date, the way real estate sites count it. It is only computed when both dates exist. A blank date is not treated as zero.
  • Price per sq ft is only computed when living_area_sqft is greater than 0 (127 rows report 0).
  • No deduplication by address. Several condo units share one street address, so each row is kept as its own sale (listing_id is the key).
  • Medians, not averages, because prices and days on market have long upper tails.
  • home_type is not used, since the data sheet says it is unreliable.
Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois–Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026. Values are as reported by the source.
